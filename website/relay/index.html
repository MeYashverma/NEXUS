What Relay is

  1. Start Relay on the phoneElevon → Labs → Relay → Start. The phone advertises over Bluetooth LE.
  2. Connect from this pageThe browser's device picker shows your phone. Pairing is standard Bluetooth; nothing is installed.
  3. Compare the 5-digit codeBoth screens show the same code, derived from the key exchange. If they match, nobody is in the middle.
  4. TypeKeys from the type box are encrypted (ECDH P-256 + AES-GCM) and inserted on the phone — wherever you enabled the Elevon Relay keyboard, or into Relay's buffer for copying.

What Relay is not

Not system-wide

This page can only capture keys typed into it, while it is focused. No web page can intercept your whole keyboard — that's the platform's security model, and Elevon won't pretend otherwise. Switching tabs pauses Relay.

Not for secrets

Text travels encrypted device-to-device, but treat Relay like any shared clipboard: avoid typing passwords or payment details on a connection you haven't code-verified.

Privacy

This page is a static file. It has no server, sends nothing anywhere, and keeps no logs. Typed text goes from this tab to your phone over Bluetooth, encrypted end-to-end; pressing STOP RELAY (or closing the tab) disconnects and clears the phone's buffer. The Elevon app itself has no internet permission. Details on the privacy page.

Troubleshooting

The picker doesn't show my phone

Start Relay on the phone first and keep its screen on. Make sure Bluetooth is on for both devices, and that the phone isn't connected to something else as an input device. On Linux, enable the Chrome experimental web platform flag.

Keys don't arrive

Relay inserts text only where the phone is looking for it: enable the Elevon Relay keyboard on the phone (Settings → System → Keyboards) and switch to it, or watch Relay's buffer on the Relay screen and copy from there. The phone screen shows RELAY ACTIVE while connected.

The codes don't match

Stop immediately and reconnect. A code mismatch means the encrypted channel isn't with the device you think it is.

It paused by itself

Relay pauses when you switch tabs or minimise the window — that's deliberate, so your typing can't be captured invisibly. Click back into the page and it resumes; use STOP RELAY to end it fully.
